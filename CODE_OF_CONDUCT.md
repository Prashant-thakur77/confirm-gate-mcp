# Code of conduct

This project is built for people whose voices are often misheard, so it should be a place where everyone is heard. That applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where the project talks to people.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new, who write in a second language, or who use assistive technology.
- Disagree about code and ideas, not about people. Assume good faith, and ask before deciding someone meant harm.
- Give feedback that someone can act on. Take feedback the same way.
- Respect people's names, pronouns and boundaries.
- Credit what others did, and own your mistakes.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Demeaning comments about anyone's disability, speech, accent, appearance, age, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, ethnicity, nationality, religion, or level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a conversation after someone has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

If something here makes you or someone else feel unwelcome or unsafe, tell the maintainer, [@Prashant-thakur77](https://github.com/Prashant-thakur77), privately, using the contact details on their GitHub profile. Don't report it in a public issue. The maintainer will reply as soon as they can. Reports are kept confidential, and the person who reports is never named without their consent.

## What happens next

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code. Depending on what happened, they may also give a private warning, ask for a temporary break from the project, or ban someone permanently. The more serious or repeated the harm, the stronger the response.

This code applies in the project's spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
